Photographs

Eighty-nine photographs

Thirty years of work, as the camera caught it: the roles on stage, the people met along the way, and the photo shoot made in Naples and on the Amalfi Coast for the debut album.

Recovered in November 2026 from the archived copies of the previous official site. The stage and backstage pictures survive only in the small size the old site published, they are shown here as they are. Photographs of the Naples shoot are by Marisa Crawford; the Metropolitan rehearsal picture of 2009 is by Marty Sohl.
