Repertoire

Roles

Lyric tenor, with a repertoire that has grown into the lirico-spinto roles. Roles sung in performance, with a selection of the houses where each was given.

Languages sung: Italian, French, Latin and Russian — the last for Lensky in Eugene Onegin at Glyndebourne. December 2026 adds English, with his first Britten: the Serenade op. 31 at the Teatro Verdi in Trieste.
