Teaching

Conservatorio di Cagliari

Giordano is Conservatory Professor in the singing class of the Conservatorio Statale di Musica “Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina” in Cagliari.

He teaches because he wants to. What he brings into the room is the thing no recording can give a student: how a role is actually built and then survived, night after night, in houses where there is no second take. He came to singing from the flute, and still teaches from inside the music: the phrasing, the breath, the line.

Everything thirty years on stage have taught him belongs, as far as he is concerned, to whoever comes next.
