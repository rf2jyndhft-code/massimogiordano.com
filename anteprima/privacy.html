Privacy and cookies

What this site does with your data

Short version: nothing is tracked unless you say yes, and saying no costs one click and is remembered.

What is stored in your browser

This site sets no advertising cookie of its own. The only thing it keeps is your answer to the banner, yes or no, saved in your browser’s local storage under the name mg-consenso. It never leaves your device and is not sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes it and the banner asks again.

You can change your answer at any time through the Cookies link at the foot of every page.

Measurement, only if you accept

If you accept, the site loads the Meta pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited). It records that a page was seen, and whether a ticket link, a contact address or a social profile was clicked. It is used to understand which pages are of interest and to run advertising on Facebook and Instagram. It sets cookies of its own and shares data with Meta, which may process it outside the European Union under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

If you decline, that script is never loaded and no request is made to Meta at all.

Video

The videos on this site are served from youtube-nocookie.com. Nothing is requested from Google until you press play.

If you write, or subscribe

Writing to info@massimogiordano.com or any of the other addresses means your message and your address are kept for as long as needed to answer and to keep a record of the exchange.

The mailing list form is not yet connected to any service, so no address can be collected through it today. When it is, you will be asked for explicit consent, told what you will receive, and every message will carry a one-click unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what is held about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to its processing, or withdraw a consent you have given. Write to info@massimogiordano.com. You may also complain to the Italian supervisory authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Who answers for this data

Data controller: Massimo Giordano.
For any request about your data, including deletion, write to info@massimogiordano.com: he answers himself.

Where the site lives. These pages are hosted by FatCow (Newfold Digital, Inc.), on servers in the United States. Like every hosting provider, it records visitors’ IP addresses and the pages requested in its server logs, to keep the site running and protect it from abuse. That means data is transferred outside the European Union, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
